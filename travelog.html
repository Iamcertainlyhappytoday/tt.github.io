    A photo themed diary.

    07-04-2024- Out of slumber, jet lag or whatever to some minor but wonderful adventure.

    The amazing warmth does suggest it could work getting out and about in a hurry. I swing in mood a lot so have to watch whether this is due to being tired or heading off on the wrong road. Many good things already to worry about but gladly.

    Tbh much is beyond me except the joy of completing some japanese phrase though this of course has more stalls in a conversation than opportunity. It is beyond to the point that I did not really hang on to the overwelming desire to describe this local landscape. I am fairly blown away by the jungle nature of the non farming and residential come urban land. I would not expect to get through anywhere here without a defined path. It is very dense but the land clearance reveals a very fine looking soil.
