Why?

My art is important to me. Organising reference collectively is very important to add method to reviewing it. In general images are very hard to manage. To be sure you have copies and other files or collections and duplicates and then when searching for a specific one it seems to be gone. It is reassuring to me that I have the thumbnails here because of this. They are strictly attached to the file and will only display if they exist exactly as stated, named and located. This can be useful for many things when you understand how images seem to detach at calling and end up somewhere else of lost completely.

Though there will be many who choose to turn a blind eye to this loss of images or manipulation of them in any case this will provide a tight reference. One of the key bases of the internet is image transfer protocol which might relate to why they move about. They are building blocks to the operating systems and the www. Very easy to use by design in a computer. This idea suits a restaurant menu don't you think. The array toggle link could go beyong the name and thumbnail to both a photo and recipe, commentary and personal tips and ideas dedicated to it?

Troubleshooting

  • If the table still has an old incorrect number it will need editing but when opening the array will be correct as shown by the correct picture.
  • Images not opening may mean somehow you have a space in the table around the name. Names being very precise do not lend to spaces which additionally might be read differently on different machines. This is why I have called the files by names without capitals and joined words together. An alternative if you like separate words and Capitals is Stella-Marz. Still watch out for spaces. Then can be ignored in the code but reintroduced elsewhere where not accounted for.
  • If you do not complete a full line there will not be an opportunity to edit so make a new line, alter the table entry number and go into the table html to delete the line. Once I am using the table I will create a way to remove a line on the browser.
